## 1. What is Virtual Studio?

**Virtual Studio** (Athena VI) is an AI avatar video creation platform. You write scripts, choose AI presenters (avatars + voices), compose multi-scene videos on a canvas and timeline, generate lip-synced presenter clips, then export a final MP4.

### What you can do

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **AI presenters** | Add talking avatars with lip-sync to each scene |
| **Multi-scene editor** | Build videos scene by scene with transitions |
| **Media & graphics** | Insert images, video, text, shapes, icons, and frames |
| **Team workspaces** | Share folders, projects, and credit pools with your team |
| **Templates** | Start from designed layouts |
| **Export** | Render and download high-quality MP4 files |

### Typical workflow (one line)

**Log in → Dashboard → Create → Canvas / Template / Details → Editor → Add scenes & media → Configure presenter → Generate → Preview → Download MP4**

## 2. Getting started — sign up & log in

### 2.1 Open the platform

1. Go to the Virtual Studio website (landing page).
2. In the top navigation, click **Log in**.

### 2.2 Log in

On the login page you can:

1. Enter your **email** and **password**, then sign in, **or**
2. Click **Continue with Google** for Google sign-in.

After a successful login you are taken to the **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`).

### 2.3 Sign up (new accounts)

1. Open **Sign up** from the auth page (or follow an invite link).
2. Create your account with email/password (or Google).
3. If you arrived via a **workspace invite**, your email may be locked to the invitation — complete signup/login to join that workspace.

### 2.4 Forgot password

1. On the login page, use the forgot-password link.
2. Follow the email instructions.
3. Open the reset link and set a new password.

### 2.5 Early access

If the product is gated, use **Request Early Access** on the landing page and submit the waitlist form. When approved, you can log in normally.

## 4. Create your first video (step by step)

Follow this path to produce a finished MP4 from scratch.

### Step 1 — Start a project

From any of these places:

- Dashboard **Home** → **Create New Video**
- Top bar → **Create**
- **Workspace** → create inside a folder
- **Templates** → use a template

The **Create a project** wizard opens.

---

### Step 2 — Canvas Size

1. Choose your video aspect ratio.
2. Currently the primary option is **16:9 Landscape** (additional ratios may show as “coming soon”).
3. Click **Next**.

---

### Step 3 — Template

1. Browse template bundles, or skip to start blank.
2. Optional filters may include categories such as **All**, **Corporate**, **Training**, **Marketing**, **Social**, **Minimal**.
3. Select a template (or skip) and continue.

---

### Step 4 — Details

Fill in project details:

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Title** | Name your project (editable later in the editor) |
| **Tags** | e.g. Professional, Presentation, Marketing, Social Media, Education, Personal |
| **Workspace** | Personal or team workspace where the project lives |
| **Folder** | Optional folder inside the workspace |

You can also **+ Create New Workspace** or **+ Create New Folder** from this step.

4. Click **Create Project**.
5. The editor opens at `/create`.

---

### Step 5 — Quick Create (blank projects)

For new blank projects, **Quick Create** often opens first:

1. **Choose your presenter** (avatar).
2. **Choose avatar look**.
3. **Select a voice** (or **Skip voice for now**).
4. **Video settings** (background, format, delivery options as shown).
5. Apply / generate into the first scene.

You can also skip and configure the presenter later in **Scene settings**.

---

### Step 6 — Build your video in the editor

1. Add and organize **scenes** (see [Section 5](#5-the-video-editor--canvas-scenes--timeline)).
2. Use **Insert** to add images, videos, text, graphics, or uploads (see [Section 6](#6-insert-tools--media)).
3. For each scene that needs a talking presenter: set avatar, voice, and script, then **Generate scene video** (see [Section 7](#7-ai-presenters--avatars-voices--generation)).
4. **Save** (auto-save runs; you can also press **Ctrl+S**).
5. **Preview**, then **Download** to export the MP4 (see [Section 8](#8-preview-save--export)).

---

### Step 7 — Find your finished video

| Type | Where to find it |
|------|------------------|
| **Draft / editable project** | **Workspace → Folders → project** |
| **Final MP4** | **My videos** and workspace **My Videos / Team Videos** |

### 5.4 Left sidebar — Scenes

The **Scenes** panel lists every scene in the project.

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| **Select a scene** | Click it to edit that scene on the canvas |
| **Add scene** | Use **+** / Add scene (may open a template picker) |
| **Duplicate scene** | Use the duplicate control on the scene |
| **Delete scene** | Use the delete control on the scene |
| **Reorder / insert between** | Use insert controls between scenes |
| **Transitions** | Set a transition between scenes |

**Transition groups** (catalog) may include: None, Dissolve, Slide, Circle Wipe, Color Wipe, Line Wipe, Match & Move, Flow, Stack, Chop.

Each scene shows a title and duration.

### 5.5 Center — Canvas

The canvas is the visual stage for the current scene (powered by Remotion):

- **Drag** layers to reposition
- **Resize** and **rotate** selected layers
- **Edit text** inline on text layers
- Use **selection overlays** and **smart guides** for alignment
- Playback can pause so you can adjust while reviewing

### 5.6 Right panel — Scene settings & properties

#### When no layer is selected — Scene settings

Configure the active scene:

| Section | What you set |
|---------|----------------|
| **Presenter & Voice** | Avatar / look, voice, narration script |
| **Generate scene video** | Create the lip-synced presenter clip for this scene |
| **Generate with new presenter** | Re-run generation with a different presenter setup |
| **Background blur** | Soften the scene background |
| **Duration** | Manual length, or **Auto from script** |
| **Voice speed** | **Slow** / **Normal** / **Fast** |
| **Scene transition** | Transition duration / behavior |

Generation status shows as processing → completed. Regenerate when you change script, voice, or avatar.

#### When a layer is selected — Layer properties

Property sections depend on layer type. Common accordion areas:

| Section | Typical controls |
|---------|------------------|
| **Position** | X/Y, size, rotation |
| **Animation** | Entrance / motion presets |
| **Layer Order** | Bring forward / send backward |
| **Fit & Adjust** | Cover / Contain / Fill; brightness & look |
| **Border & Frame** | Stroke, frames |
| **Fill & Style / Visual** | Colors, opacity, styling |
| **Source / Image Fill** | Media source and fill mode |
| **Set as background / Scene Background** | Promote media to background |
| **Avatar** | Avatar-specific options when relevant |
| **Shape & Style** | For graphic / shape layers |

**Text layers** typically include: font family, size, weight, Bold / Italic / Underline / Strikethrough, alignment (left / center / right), and colors.

**Audio layers** typically include: playback, volume & fade, timing, and actions.

#### Animation presets (examples)

Fade, Slide up/down, Zoom in; Pop, Wipe, Blur, Succession, Breathe, Baseline, Drift, Tectonic, Tumble, Neon, Scrapbook, Stomp; Rotate, Flicker, Pulse, Wiggle.

#### Fit & Flip

- Fit: **Cover** / **Contain** / **Fill**
- Flip horizontal/vertical
- Looks: Normal / Bright / Vivid / Muted / Drama
- Sliders: Brightness, Contrast, and related adjustments

### 5.7 Bottom — Timeline

The **timeline** shows layers over time for the current scene or the full project.

Typical timeline controls:

| Control | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Undo / Redo | History |
| Copy / Duplicate | Duplicate selected layers |
| Bring forward / Send backward | Layer stacking |
| Play / Pause | Preview playback |
| Full timeline | Expand from single-scene scope to full project |
| Zoom Out / Zoom In / Fit timeline | Timeline scale |
| Add Scene / Delete scene | Scene management |
| Track up / down | Move tracks |

## 6. Insert tools & media

Open **Insert** from the editor top bar.

### 6.1 Images

1. Choose **Insert → Images**.
2. Browse stock / workspace images.
3. Click or drag an image onto the canvas.
4. Adjust position, fit, and animation in the properties panel.

### 6.2 Videos

1. Choose **Insert → Videos**.
2. Browse available video clips (stock or workspace).
3. Place on the canvas / timeline and trim timing as needed.

### 6.3 Upload

1. Choose **Insert → Upload**.
2. Select files from your device.
3. Uploads are saved to the **workspace** linked to the project.

**Supported types:** JPEG, PNG, WebP, MP4, MP3  
**Max size:** 50 MB per file

Wait until uploads finish before exporting. Assets are linked by ID in the project so they remain available after save.

### 6.4 Text

1. Choose **Insert → Text**.
2. Pick a text preset or add a text layer.
3. Edit copy on the canvas.
4. Style fonts, size, weight, alignment, and color in the text properties panel.

### 6.5 Graphics

**Insert → Graphics** includes tabs:

| Tab | Contents |
|-----|----------|
| **Shapes** | Geometric and decorative shapes (categories may include All, Shapes, Lines, Basic, Polygons, Stars, Arrows, Flowchart, Bubbles, Clouds, Pitch, Launch, Course, Sales, Social) |
| **Icons** | Icon sets (e.g. Business, Commerce, Contact, Growth, …) |
| **Frames** | Picture frames and cutouts (e.g. Basic, Film, Devices, Paper, Flowers, Blob, Retro) |

Place a graphic, then style it under **Shape & Style** / related property sections.

### 6.6 Stock media tip

Stock items imported in the editor become workspace assets (often with a **Stock** badge in the Library) and count toward storage.

## 7. AI presenters — avatars, voices & generation

Presenters are configured **per scene**.

### 7.1 Set presenter & voice for a scene

1. Select a scene in the left **Scenes** list.
2. With no layer selected, open **Scene settings** on the right.
3. Under **Presenter & Voice**:
   - Choose a **presenter** (avatar) and **look**
   - Choose a **voice**
   - Enter the **narration script**
4. Optionally set **Voice speed** (Slow / Normal / Fast) and **Duration** (or **Auto from script**).

You can also start this via **Quick Create** when opening a blank project.

### 7.2 Generate the scene presenter clip

1. Confirm avatar, voice, and script are set.
2. Click **Generate scene video** (or **Generate with new presenter** if replacing).
3. Wait until status shows **completed** (generation continues in the background).
4. Repeat for **every scene** that should have a talking presenter.

> **Important:** Final export may wait for all avatar scene clips to finish before stitching the full MP4.

### 7.3 Manage avatars & voices outside the editor

- **Dashboard → Avatars** — browse public library, your avatars, team shared; create new avatars  
- **Dashboard → Voices** — browse / create / clone / design voices  

See [Sections 11–12](#11-avatars-library) for full details.

### 7.4 Credits

Avatar and voice generation consume **credits** from your personal balance or the active workspace pool. Watch the credits bar in the editor top bar.

## 8. Preview, save & export

### 8.1 Save

- Auto-save runs while you work.
- Manual save: click **Save** or press **Ctrl+S**.
- Drafts live under **Workspace → Folders**.

### 8.2 Preview

1. Click **Preview** in the top bar.
2. Review scenes, layers, transitions, and presenter clips.
3. Close the preview and fix anything needed.

### 8.3 Export / Download MP4

1. Click **Download** (or press **Ctrl+E**).
2. In the **Download video** modal, set:
   - **Filename**
   - **Resolution** (e.g. 1080p / 720p / 4K)
   - **Quality** (High / Medium / Low)
3. Confirm to start the server render.

**Render phases:** Saving → Queued → Rendering → Preparing → Ready

4. When ready, download the MP4 to your device.
5. The finished file also appears under **My videos** and the workspace video library.

### 8.4 Export checklist

Before exporting, confirm:

- [ ] All scenes look correct on the canvas
- [ ] All needed presenter clips show **completed**
- [ ] Uploads finished (no pending uploads)
- [ ] You have enough **credits**
- [ ] Workspace has enough **storage** for the render

### 8.5 Delivery note

Virtual Studio delivers the finished video as a **downloadable MP4**. There is no separate one-click publish to YouTube/social inside the editor — share the file after download.

## 9. Workspaces, folders & collaboration

### 9.1 Personal vs team workspaces

| Type | Who can access | Credits / storage |
|------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Private (personal)** | Only you | Your personal credits & quota |
| **Team workspace** | Invited members | Shared **workspace credit pool** and owner storage quota |

In **Workspace**, you may see groups such as **My Workspaces** and **Shared with Me**.

### 9.2 Folders and projects

1. Open **Workspace** from the sidebar.
2. Select a workspace.
3. Open or create a **folder**.
4. Click a **video project** to reopen it in the editor.

Projects are **editable timelines**. Final MP4s are separate (see My Videos).

### 9.3 Project actions

From a folder, you can typically:

- **Rename** a project
- **Move to Folder**
- **Delete** a project (removes the draft timeline; past exports may remain until cleaned up separately)

### 9.4 Invite members & roles

Team owners can invite collaborators by email and assign:

| Role | Typical access |
|------|----------------|
| **Owner** | Full control; holds credit pool and storage quota |
| **Admin** | Manage contributors and workspace resources |
| **Member** | Work in shared folders when permitted |

Credits for renders in a team workspace are drawn from the **workspace pool** allocated by the owner.

### 9.5 Workspace videos (exports tab)

Inside a workspace, open **My Videos** or **Team Videos** for **completed renders** in that workspace.

- Owners often see **My Videos** (exports count toward their storage).
- Members see **Team Videos**; **Rendered by** shows who exported.

### 9.6 Project comments

In the editor, open **Project comments** to leave notes and **@mention** teammates. Notification deep-links can take you back to the relevant comment.

| Pool | Used for |

## 18. FAQ

**Where are my finished videos?**  
Completed MP4s appear on **My videos** and under the workspace **My Videos / Team Videos** tab. Drafts stay in **Workspace → Folders**.

**Where are my drafts?**  
**Workspace →** select workspace → open a **folder** → click the project to reopen the editor.

**Why did my upload fail with a storage error?**  
The workspace owner exceeded quota. Delete unused assets or old exports, or ask an administrator for more storage.

**Why can’t I delete an asset?**  
It may still be used in a project, or you may lack permission in a team workspace. Remove it from projects first, or ask the uploader, owner, or admin.

**Do team member exports show for the owner?**  
Yes. Completed renders appear in the shared workspace library. **Rendered by** is attribution only.

**What file types can I upload?**  
JPEG, PNG, WebP, MP4, and MP3 up to **50 MB** per file.

**Why is export waiting?**  
Presenter scene generation may still be running. Wait until each needed scene shows **completed**, then export again.

**How do I get help?**  
Open **Help** in the dashboard, or email **support@athenavi.com** with your workspace name, project title, and steps to reproduce. Include screenshots or export IDs for render issues.
